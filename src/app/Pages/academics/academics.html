OUR APPROACH

Our Academic Approach

Our approach to teaching is centred on the individual learner. We recognise that every child is unique, with different abilities, interests and learning needs.

Our teachers use a combination of proven teaching methods, classroom activities, practical experiences and appropriate technology to make learning meaningful and engaging. We encourage pupils and students to ask questions, think independently, solve problems and apply what they learn to real-life situations.

We also maintain high academic standards while ensuring that our students develop the confidence, discipline and values needed to become responsible members of society.
