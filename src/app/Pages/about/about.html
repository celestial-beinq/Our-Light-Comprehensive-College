A Word From Our Principal

"At Our Light Comprehensive College, we believe every child is endowed with unique brilliance. Our mission is to ignite that potential through disciplined scholarship, personal mentorship, and a global worldview."

Founded with the vision to raise leaders of intellect and character, our institution offers an environment where young men and women discover their passions. Whether in our state-of-the-art robotics laboratory, our Olympic-standard sports arena, or through the eloquence of our debating chambers, we prepare students not merely to pass examinations, but to conquer life.

We celebrate our consistent 100% pass rates in WAEC, NECO, and Cambridge IGCSE, but we take equal pride in our alumni who demonstrate moral courage, empathy, and entrepreneurial zeal wherever they go.
